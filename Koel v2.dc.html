III generations of one stubborn, coffee-mad family
Our grandfather roasted the first batch on a hand-crank drum in Chikmagalur in 1972 and rang a bicycle bell when it was ready. His daughter built the estate partnerships. Her daughter brought the drum north to Gurugram — and cups every lot from eleven estates across India before it ships. The bell still rings on Tuesdays.

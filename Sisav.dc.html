The ritual
Filter, dabarah, tumbler — poured the old way.
मीटर कॉफ़ी
From Chikmagalur to the Nilgiri hills, filter coffee is made the same unhurried way it always was — a brass filter, a steel tumbler, and a wrist that knows the pour. Mysore Nuggets Extra Bold sits in the same tradition, kept for those who take it strong.
01 — Rest
Coarse-ground beans and chicory steep overnight in a brass filter, one slow drip at a time.
02 — Boil
Milk is brought to a gentle boil, never rushed, skimmed of its skin.
03 — Pour
Decoction, milk, and sugar move dabarah to tumbler and back, a long unbroken pour, until froth rises.

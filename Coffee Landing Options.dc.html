The process
Four decisions,
made carefully.
01 — Source
Direct relationships with nine farms. We pay 2.4× the commodity price, every harvest.
02 — Roast
Profiled by hand on a 1958 Probat. First crack logged to the second; development held under 18%.
03 — Rest
Seven days of degassing before any bag ships. Roast date printed, never a best-before.
04 — Brew
A recipe card in every box: dose, ratio, temperature, time. Nothing left to guesswork.
